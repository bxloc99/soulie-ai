How do I restore my VIP purchase?

Open Settings or the VIP screen and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone go to Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Soulie AI and tap Cancel. Deleting the App does not cancel the subscription.

My companion is not talking or replying.

Check your internet connection and that microphone and speech recognition permissions are enabled in iOS Settings. If voice audio fails, the App automatically switches to the device voice.

How do I delete my data?

Use Clear chat history in Settings, or delete the App to remove everything stored on your device. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I report an inappropriate reply?

Long-press the message and choose Report, or email us with a screenshot.
