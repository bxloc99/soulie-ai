Privacy Policy

Soulie AI: 3D Companion · Effective date: October 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Soulie AI (“the App”, “we”, “us”) collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. The App is operated by Bui Loc. If you have questions, contact us at bxloc871999@gmail.com.

We do not require you to create an account, we do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your data to track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

1. Information you provide

  • Profile details you enter during setup or in Settings: nickname, date of birth (used to compute your age), gender, interests, and how you and your companion address each other.
  • Messages you type or speak to your companion, plus requests for tarot readings and interactive stories.
  • Voice input when you use the microphone. Speech is converted to text using Apple’s Speech framework (on-device where your device supports it). We do not store audio recordings.
  • Photos you capture in the in-app studio. These are saved to your Photo Library only when you choose to save them.

2. Information collected automatically

  • Usage data: screens viewed and in-app actions (for example, choosing a character, a dance, or a theme) and the length — not the content — of messages you send. Collected with Google Firebase Analytics.
  • Diagnostics: crash reports and technical error information (device model, OS version, app version). Collected with Firebase Crashlytics.
  • App integrity: Firebase App Check and Apple App Attest verify that requests come from a genuine copy of the App.
  • Network data: like any internet service, the servers the App contacts receive your IP address.

3. How your information is used

  • To generate your companion’s replies, tarot readings and stories, personalised with your profile details.
  • To turn your companion’s replies into spoken audio.
  • To unlock and restore VIP features you purchase.
  • To understand how the App is used, fix crashes and improve features.

4. Sharing with third-party AI and service providers

To provide the features above, the App sends some of your data to the following providers. They process it on our behalf or under their own terms, and we do not permit them to sell it.

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of users.

5. Storage and retention

  • Your profile, chat history, tarot journal and stories are stored locally on your device. They are removed when you clear them in Settings or delete the App.
  • Our AI relay server forwards requests to AI providers and does not store conversation content.
  • Analytics and crash data are kept by Firebase according to Google’s retention settings (up to 14 months for Analytics, 90 days for Crashlytics).

6. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your data: use “Clear chat history” and the other reset options in Settings, or delete the App. Because there is no account, there is nothing else for us to delete on our side beyond the provider data described above.
  • Permissions: you can turn off microphone, speech recognition and photo access at any time in iOS Settings.
  • Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct or delete your information. Contact us at bxloc871999@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

7. Children

The App is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has used the App, contact us and we will help remove any related data.

8. AI-generated content

Your companion is an AI character. Its replies are generated automatically, can be inaccurate, and are not professional, medical, legal or financial advice. Tarot readings are for entertainment only.

9. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Sensitive settings are stored in the iOS Keychain. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be announced in the App or on this page, with a new effective date.
